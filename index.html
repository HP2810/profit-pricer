Selling price is solved so profit clears your target after eBay's final value fee, per-order fee, buyer-side shipping economics, and sales-tax pass-through — then rounded up to a psychological ending, never down. Market price (when you supply it) only ever nudges the price down toward competitiveness, never below your profit floor. "Live market check" opens eBay's own sold & completed listings for the product, matched on name and condition (new items get eBay's "New" filter) — it does not try to match shipping terms. Title suggestions follow eBay's published Best Match ranking guidance (front-loaded keywords, full item-specific coverage, no banned filler); Cassini itself is a proprietary black box no outside tool can query directly, so treat these as strong, rules-built starting points rather than a guarantee. Real eBay fee rates vary by category and store subscription — verify yours in Seller Hub.
